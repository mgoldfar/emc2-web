Prashant J. Nair is a Senior Principal Engineer at d-matrix Inc. and an Assistant Professor at the University of British Columbia (on sabbatical/leave). His research focuses on computer architecture and systems for AI/ML. His honors include the 2024 TCCA Young Architect Award, the 2025 DSN Test of Time Award, the HPCA 2023 Best Paper Award, a MICRO 2024 Best Paper nomination, and the HPCA 2025 Distinguished Artifact Award. He has published 45+ top-tier papers, and as of 2025, is the only Assistant Professor named to all three premier computer architecture Halls of Fame: ISCA, MICRO, and HPCA.
