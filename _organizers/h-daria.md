Daria Soboleva is a Head Research Scientist at Cerebras Systems, where she designed Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) training recipes for wafer-scale hardware and now leads model training at unprecedented scale. Previously, she built ML systems used by millions at Google and Yandex, where she invented the YATI model powering Yandex Search. She received her degree from Moscow State University.
