Ananya Pareek works in processor and system performance, spanning CPU, GPU, SoC and full-platform architecture at Apple, Google, Samsung, MediaTek and MIPS, applied recently to machine learning systems including large language model inference on datacenter GPU platforms. His interests are in hardware platforms for machine learning, hardware/software co-design, and simulation methodology for architectural exploration. He received his M.S. from the University of Rochester and B.Tech. from Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur.
